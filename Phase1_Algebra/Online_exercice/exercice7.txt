Sur la figure ci-contre, le carré ABCD a pour côté (2x + 3) centimètres.
Afin d'obtenir une bande de 1cm de large, on découpe un petit carré à
l'intérieur du grand carré.
Exprimer l'aire de la bande grise en fonction de x.

première correction fausse:
A = 4(2x + 3) - 4((2x + 3) - 1)

deuxième correction:
A = (2x + 3)² - (2x + 1)²
A = (4x² + 12x + 9) - (4x² + 4x + 1)
A = 4x² + 12x + 9 - 4x² - 4x - 1
A = 12x + 9 - 4x - 1
A = 8x + 8

The Setup:
A family wants to build a square swimming pool in their backyard.
They want a paved walkway that is exactly 2 meters wide all the way around the pool.
Let the side length of the square pool itself be x meters.
Your Task:
Find the simplified algebraic expression for the total area of the paved walkway
only in terms of x.

A = (x + 4)² - x²

Challenge 2:
The Setup: Think of any number.
1. Add 3 to it.
2. Square the result.
3. Subtract the square of the original number.
4. Subtract 9.
Your Task:
Translate this game into an algebraic expression using a variable 'n'
Expand and simplify it to find the "secret rule" of the game.
What does this trick always do to your original number?

x = (n + 3)² - n² - 9
x = (n² + 6n + 9) - n² - 9
x = 6n
