The problem
A square has a side length of 2x + 3.
Its area is therefore:

1: Rewrite the square as a multiplication of two identical factors.
2: Develop the expression using distributivity.
3: Simplify the result.
4: Calculate the area when x=2.
5: If the area is 49 and x≥0, find x.

1: (2x + 3)(2x + 3)
2: 4x² + 6x + 6x + 9
3: 4x² + 12x + 9
4: (2(2) + 3)² = (7)² = 49
5: x = 2
