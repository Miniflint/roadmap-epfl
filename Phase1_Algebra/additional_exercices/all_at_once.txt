The problem
A square has a side length of:
L = (2x + 6)/2
It's area:
E = L²

1: Simplify the expression for L.
2: Write the area E in terms of x.
3: Develop and simplify the expression for E.
4: Calculate the area when x=3.
5: Find the nonnegative side length if the area is 25.
6: Find x when the area is 25, given that x≥0.
7: Verify your result by substituting x into the original expression for L.

1: L = x + 3
2: E = (x + 3)²
3: E = (x + 3)(x + 3) = x² + 6x + 9
4: (3)² + 6(3) + 9 = 9 + 18 + 9 = 36 | E = 36
5: E = 25 | L = sqrt(25) | L = 5
6: E = 25 | L = sqrt(E) | x + 3 = 5 | x = 2
7: (2(2) + 6)/2 = 5 | (4 + 6)/2 = 5 | 10/2 = 5 | 5 = 5
