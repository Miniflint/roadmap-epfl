The situation
Imagine a large square garden. Inside it, there is a smaller square pond that you want to build.
The garden has a side length of:
L = (2x + 6)/2 + sqrt(9/4)

l = sqrt((x + 1)²) + (x - 3)/2

The pond is entirely inside the garden.
The area of the garden that isn't covered by the pond is the remaining area.
Your goal is to determine the value of x for which the remaining area is exactly 48 square units.
Assume x≥3.
The remaining area of the garden, excluding the pond, is 48 square units.

14: Verify that the remaining area is exactly 48 square units.
