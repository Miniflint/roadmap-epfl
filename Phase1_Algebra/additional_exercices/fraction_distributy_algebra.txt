The problem
Consider the expression:
D = (3x + 6)/3 − (x − 2)/2

1: Simplify the first fraction.
2: Simplify the second fraction as much as possible.
3: Find a common denominator.
4: Simplify the complete expression.
5: Solve the equation: D=5
6: Check your solution in the original equation.

1: (x + 2)/1 = x + 2
2: the second fraction cannot be simplified more than (x − 2)/2
3: common denominator is 2 | (x + 2)/1 = (2x + 4)/2
4: (3x + 6)/3 − (x − 2)/2 = (2x + 4)/2 - (x − 2)/2 = (2x - x + 4 - (-2))/2 = (x + 6)/2
5: 5 = (x + 6)/2 | 10 = x + 6 | 4 = x | x = 4
6: (3(4) + 6)/3 − (4 − 2)/2 = 5 | (12 + 6)/3 - 2/2 = 5 | 18/3 - 1 = 5 | 6 - 1 = 5 | 5 = 5
